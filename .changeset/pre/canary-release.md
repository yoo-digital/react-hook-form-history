First canary release.

- `@yoo-digital/rhf-history`: undo/redo for react-hook-form, built for large forms. `useFormHistory`, `useFormHistoryState` and `useUndoRedoHotkeys`, plus the framework-free history and `merge3` in `/core`.
- `@yoo-digital/rhf-history-persist`: drafts that survive a reload, with an IndexedDB adapter in `/indexeddb`, expiry, cross-tab clearing on logout, and recovery of drafts across document revisions.
